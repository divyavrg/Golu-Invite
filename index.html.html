Know about these traditions

🌸
Nine nights of ThayarIn Sri Vaishnava homes, these nights are for Sri Mahalakshmi, our Thayar, beside Perumal.
🏺
Why a kalasham in a silver sombu?It is the sacred seat of the Goddess for these nine days. The sombu holds water, topped with mango leaves and a coconut, and is worshipped as the presence of Sri Mahalakshmi. Silver is seen as pure and auspicious.
🎎
Perumal, Thayar & the DasavatharamPadis are always odd in number. The deities, such as Perumal and Thayar, take the top steps, with the ten avatars of Vishnu, temples and scenes of everyday life on the steps below.
📿
Evenings of devotionEvenings often bring Sri Suktam, Lakshmi Ashtothram and pasurams from the Divya Prabandham.
🥣
Sundal & thamboolamA different sundal each evening, and every guest leaves with thamboolam: kumkum, manjal, betel and a fruit.
📖
Saraswati & Ayudha Pooja · 19 OctBooks, instruments and tools are placed for blessings. Vijayadasami follows on 20 Oct, when the golu is gently put to rest.
